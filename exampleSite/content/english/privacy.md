# Privacy

MSA is a secure, private peer-support community. We prioritise privacy, discretion, and member anonymity.

privacy and anonymity are taken seriously. We aim to minimise data collection wherever possible and avoid unnecessary tracking, analytics, or invasive monitoring on our website and community platforms.

To maintain a safe environment, staff may discuss moderation and safety matters in private staff channels, which can occasionally include usernames, warnings, bans, or verification notes. Outside of necessary moderation and safety processes, member privacy is respected as much as possible.

To help protect member anonymity and community safety, MSA staff may remove personal information shared within public community spaces if it appears to have been posted accidentally or if it creates a safety or privacy risk for the member or broader community.

## What we keep private

* We do not share logs, screenshots, or member identities outside our community.
* We do not require real names or personal details unless you choose to share them.&#x20;
* We do not track any Data from the use of our website or Chat rooms.

## Vetting & onboarding

Access is managed through email-based vetting to protect members and keep the space safe and lawful. We encourge you to use a secure email that dose not tie back to your personal infomation when sending an email to staff.

## Content boundaries

We do not allow explicit sexual content or anything illegal.

# Essential OPSEC Guide

Because this is the online world, you can never be completely certain who is behind another screen. While staff carefully review applications and community behaviour, you should still take steps to protect your own privacy and digital footprint.

### Clean Your Links

When sharing links from platforms such as YouTube, TikTok, or Twitter/X, remove tracking text at the end of the URL (for example, anything beginning with `?si=` or `?ref=`). These tracking tags can sometimes connect back to your personal accounts or browsing activity.

### Use a VPN

Consider using a trusted VPN alongside a secure browser setup to improve your privacy and reduce unnecessary exposure of your IP address or location data.

### Limit Personal Information

Avoid sharing your real name, workplace, address, school, or highly specific personal details. Only share information you would be comfortable potentially becoming public.

### Guard Your DMs

Staff cannot monitor or control private messages or one-on-one conversations between members. Be cautious about what you share privately, especially with people you do not know well.
