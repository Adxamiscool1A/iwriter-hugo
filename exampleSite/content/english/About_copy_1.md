# Welcome to MSA

MSA is a dedicated, secure Australian-based chat community for Minor Attracted people commonly Abbreviated to MAPS. We provide a calm, respectful, and non-judgmental environment where members can connect with others who understand similar experiences, receive peer support, and reduce isolation.

Our community mainly operates through a dedicated encrypted online chat space focused on general conversation, mutual support, and healthy connection. MSA is strictly an 18+ community and operates fully within Australian law.

***

## Why We Exist

For many people, discovering they experience these attractions can be an isolating and frightening experience. Feelings of shame, fear, loneliness, and self-hatred often lead people to suffer in silence, believing they have nowhere they can speak honestly or be understood without immediate judgment.

MSA exists to reduce that isolation.

We believe that nobody should have to navigate these thoughts and experiences completely alone. Our goal is simple: to provide a calm, respectful, and supportive environment where members can speak openly, connect with others who understand similar struggles, and support one another in our day to day challenges.

At its core, MSA is about human connection, peer support, privacy, and creating a space where people can feel heard without fear or judgement from those around us.

***

## Our Core Pillars

### Privacy & Discretion

Member privacy is extremely important to us. Our spaces are designed to support anonymity and provide a secure environment for respectful discussion and support.

### Respectful Community

We aim to maintain a calm, welcoming, and moderated community built around mutual respect, constructive conversation, and peer support.

### Safety & Legality

MSA operates strictly within Australian State and Federal laws. We maintain a zero-tolerance policy toward illicit material, harmful behaviour, or anything that may place the community or its members at risk.
