# Welcome to MSA

MSA is an Australian-based,  Secure, private peer-support community. We provide a safe, legal, and non-judgmental environment for Minnor attracted people to connect, recive peer support, and reduce isolation.

Our focus is entirely on Mutual respect, general connection, and supporting other Maps within Australia.

### Our Core Pillars

* **100% Legally Compliant:** We operate strictly within the bounds of Australian State and Federal laws. We maintain a firm zero-tolerance policy for any illicit material or harmful behaviors.
* **Commitment to Privacy:** Your safety and discretion are our highest priorities. Our spaces are designed to support user anonymity and protect personal privacy.
* **Respectful Community:** We provide a calm, supportive, and moderated online space focused on constructive peer interaction. MSA is strictly an **18+ community**.
