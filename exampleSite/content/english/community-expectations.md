## Safety & Legality
All members must follow Australian State and Federal laws at all times. We maintain a zero-tolerance policy toward illicit material or the encouragement of harmful behaviour.

## Mutual Respect
Treat everyone with kindness. Harassment, bullying, aggressive behaviour, or discrimination (including racism and sexism) have no place here.

## Privacy & Discretion
What is shared within our community stays within our community. Do not share logs, screenshots, or member identities externally.

## Server Organization
Keep conversations in the relevant channels and use only one account within the community.

## Staff Support
Our moderation team is here to keep the space running smoothly. Follow direction from staff and raise concerns constructively and respectfully.
